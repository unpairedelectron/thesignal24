---
name: Situation-room evidence boundary
description: Evidence rule for connecting current caste and reservation events to daily decisions and migration pressure.
---

The information hub may explain how a verified record could affect decisions about study, work, applications, staying, speaking, or leaving. It must not present migration, exit plans, or cross-border movement as a measured trend unless the publication has a source-backed record that supports the claim.

For reservation coverage, distinguish “open” competition from category-specific reservation: open seats are not an exclusive upper-caste pool. Attribute concerns about unequal protection or misuse to petitioners, protesters, or other named actors unless a court or primary source establishes the claim. Do not use “Modi Act” as a legal title without identifying an exact statute; use the formal 103rd Amendment / EWS terminology when that is the intended reference.

**Why:** The user wants an incisive infographic experience, but the publication’s credibility depends on distinguishing lived pressure from quantified evidence.

**How to apply:** Use explicit evidence-status labels and visible gaps. Add migration or decision indicators only through source-linked editorial records with geography, date, definition, and verification context. For category and quota claims, link the current rule or order and state who is making each disputed claim.
