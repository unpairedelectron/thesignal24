---
name: Reservation archive editorial rule
description: Product and editorial constraints for the India-wide caste-based reservation archive.
---

The reservation archive should separate reporting, legal/policy records, historical documents, paper notes, explainers, and ground reporting. Every record should make its source, publication date, region/state, and verification status visible, and starter content must not be presented as breaking news.

**Why:** Caste and reservation are sensitive, high-stakes topics where decontextualized claims can inflame rather than inform. The product’s value is helping readers verify and remember the record.

**How to apply:** Preserve calm, precise language in UI and editorial workflows. Prefer primary documents and reputable reporting, keep current reporting distinct from historical material, and add ingestion/moderation before claiming comprehensive live coverage.
