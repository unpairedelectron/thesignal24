---
name: Public archive imagery
description: How this publication should source documentary visuals when image-search tooling is unavailable.
---

Use identifiable public-archive imagery for historical and documentary visuals, and keep the source or credit visible in the interface. Do not use an image of a real event as if it proves a different story.

**Why:** The publication’s subject is politically and socially sensitive; visual context can mislead readers as easily as a headline can.

**How to apply:** Prefer Wikimedia Commons or another traceable public archive, retain the original file/page attribution when downloading assets, and label images as archive visuals unless they are directly tied to the record being reported.
