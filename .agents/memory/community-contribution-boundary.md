---
name: Community contribution boundary
description: Product boundary for participant-created discussion content in The Signal 24.
---

Participant-created thoughts, articles, and creative pieces are a separate public discussion layer. They must not appear in the verified editorial archive or be presented as Signal 24 reporting without an explicit editorial review step.

**Why:** The publication is source-first, and community participation should broaden discussion without weakening the distinction between lived contributions and checked records.

**How to apply:** Keep community routes, labels, and data models distinct from editorial records. Any future promotion into the archive should be an explicit moderation/editorial workflow with its own attribution and verification state.
