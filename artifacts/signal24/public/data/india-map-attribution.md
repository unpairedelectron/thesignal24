India state and union-territory boundary data: DataMeet India community and the Election Commission of India, distributed by geoBoundaries.

- Dataset: geoBoundaries India ADM1, boundary ID `IND-ADM1-1811400`
- Dataset metadata and downloads: https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/IND/ADM1/
- Underlying source: https://github.com/datameet/maps
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 2.5 India (CC BY 2.5 IN): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/in/
- Dataset metadata lists boundary year represented as 2011 and source-data update date as January 19, 2023.

Changes for this map:

- Source `shapeName` values are retained in each feature; `NAME_1` uses unaccented English administrative names for matching records.
- Geometry is simplified with a 0.01-degree tolerance and coordinates are rounded to five decimal places for editorial map display.
- The previous map's legacy labels resolve to current names: Orissa → Odisha, Uttaranchal → Uttarakhand, and Andaman and Nicobar → Andaman and Nicobar Islands.
- The former Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu features are represented together as the current Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu union territory.

The map is for editorial navigation only, not a legal boundary reference. Administrative boundaries and territorial claims can be disputed or change; verify against current authoritative publications before making boundary claims.
