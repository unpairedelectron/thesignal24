Redesign the website at https://signal-hub.replit.app/ as a premium, serious, investigative digital publication called “Signal Hub”.

CORE DESIGN DIRECTION

Create a website that feels:
- authoritative
- investigative
- intelligent
- editorial
- modern
- evidence-driven
- visually powerful
- trustworthy
- serious without becoming depressing
- premium enough to feel like a major independent research publication

Do NOT make it look like:
- a generic SaaS startup
- a fintech dashboard
- a crypto website
- a corporate consulting site
- a typical Indian news portal
- a flashy AI-generated website
- a social-media/blog template

Think visually somewhere between:
- The New York Times
- Reuters Graphics
- Bloomberg editorial interfaces
- The Economist
- ProPublica
- modern data-journalism websites
- premium investigative research platforms

The website should communicate:
“Here are the facts. Here is the evidence. Here is the history. Here is what changed.”

BRAND IDENTITY

Brand name:
SIGNAL HUB

Possible tagline:
“Follow the evidence.”
Alternative:
“Facts. Context. Accountability.”

Logo:
Create a sophisticated minimal wordmark.
Use a subtle signal/wave/document-inspired symbol.
Avoid generic AI logos, globes, megaphones, political party symbols, or stock-news icons.

COLOR SYSTEM

Use a restrained editorial palette.

Primary:
- near-black / charcoal
- warm off-white
- paper white

Accent:
- deep crimson or dark red for important alerts, controversies and breaking developments
- muted amber for warnings / pending developments
- restrained blue for links, data and analytical elements

Do not use neon gradients.

Avoid excessive pure black backgrounds.
Most reading surfaces should feel like premium paper/editorial layouts.

TYPOGRAPHY

Use a sophisticated serif font for major editorial headlines.

Suggested:
- Playfair Display
- Georgia
- Libre Baskerville
- Source Serif
- Cormorant Garamond

Use a clean sans-serif for:
- navigation
- metadata
- labels
- statistics
- buttons
- filters
- interface elements

Suggested:
- Inter
- IBM Plex Sans
- Geist
- Source Sans 3

Create a strong typographic hierarchy.

HEADLINE:
48–72px desktop

SECTION HEADLINE:
32–42px

ARTICLE HEADLINE:
32–56px

BODY:
17–20px with generous line height

Small metadata:
11–13px uppercase with letter spacing
