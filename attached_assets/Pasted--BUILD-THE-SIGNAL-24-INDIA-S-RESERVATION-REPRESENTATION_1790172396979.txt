# BUILD THE SIGNAL 24 — INDIA'S RESERVATION & REPRESENTATION MONITOR

Build a production-grade, premium, editorial + data platform for:

**Domain:** https://thesignal24.com
**Brand:** The Signal 24
**Descriptor:** India's Reservation & Representation Monitor
**Core tagline:** **The record behind the debate.**

---

# 1. PRODUCT VISION

The Signal 24 is a public-interest digital publication and research/archive platform focused exclusively on:

* caste-based reservation
* affirmative action
* reservation policy
* representation
* government recruitment reservation
* educational reservation
* promotions and reservation
* constitutional amendments
* parliamentary developments
* Supreme Court and High Court cases
* commissions and committee reports
* government notifications and gazette documents
* reservation-related protests and movements
* historical developments
* statistical evidence
* implementation outcomes
* policy criticism and analysis

The goal is NOT to become another generic news website.

The goal is to create a **permanent, searchable, authoritative record of India's reservation debate**.

The site should feel like a combination of:

* premium investigative publication
* data journalism platform
* legal/policy archive
* searchable research database
* modern Indian digital magazine

The product should be visually iconic, extremely fast, mobile-first and credible.

---

# 2. EDITORIAL PRINCIPLE

The site must be:

**evidence-first, government-independent, source-first and transparent.**

Do NOT design the editorial system around supporting or opposing any political party, caste group or reservation policy.

The publication may strongly criticize policies, decisions, implementation failures, contradictions or political claims, but criticism must be based on documented evidence.

Never present an allegation as an established fact.

Never manufacture statistics.

Never infer motives without reliable sourcing.

Never fabricate quotations, government statements, court observations or research findings.

Political parties and governments must be treated under the same editorial framework.

The site should be able to critically investigate:

* BJP
* Congress
* regional parties
* central government
* state governments
* courts
* commissions
* universities
* recruiting bodies
* political movements
* public institutions

without changing the underlying editorial standard.

Clearly distinguish:

**REPORTING**
What happened.

**DOCUMENT**
What the original source says.

**ANALYSIS**
What the available evidence suggests.

**OPINION**
A clearly labelled editorial viewpoint.

Do not blur these categories.

# 57. FINAL QUALITY BAR

Before considering the implementation complete, ask:

1. Does this look like a serious national publication?
2. Can a reader trace a major claim to a source?
3. Can a reader understand what changed historically?
4. Can a reader find relevant court cases?
5. Can a reader find the original government document?
6. Can a reader compare states?
7. Can a reader distinguish reporting from opinion?
8. Can a reader identify what is confirmed versus disputed?
9. Is the site extremely fast on mobile?
10. Does the site provide substantially more value than a normal news search?

If the answer to any of these is no, continue improving the implementation.
