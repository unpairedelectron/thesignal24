Now make Signal Hub significantly more India-first, visual, engaging and image-led.

The evidence architecture that was just implemented must remain intact.

Do NOT remove:

- Article → Event → Source relationships
- explicit source records
- evidence pages
- archive
- timeline
- data room
- source status
- editorial audit information

Instead, redesign the PRESENTATION LAYER so Signal Hub feels like a premium Indian digital publication rather than a research database.

The biggest problem to solve:

The current design is too text-heavy and academically structured.

Signal Hub needs to immediately attract an Indian internet user visually while maintaining credibility.

Think:

PREMIUM INDIAN INVESTIGATIVE JOURNALISM
+
MODERN DIGITAL MAGAZINE
+
DATA JOURNALISM
+
EVIDENCE ARCHIVE

   ==================================================

The website should feel unmistakably relevant to India.

Use visual references such as:

- Indian cities
- Indian streets
- government buildings
- Parliament
- Supreme Court / High Courts
- state secretariats
- universities
- public institutions
- newspapers and documents
- maps of India
- state maps
- archival photographs
- official notices
- court documents
- policy documents
- public demonstrations when editorially relevant
- ordinary Indian citizens and communities
- infrastructure
- education
- employment
- public services

Do NOT turn the site into a stereotypical "India" design.

Avoid:

- excessive saffron/green/orange
- Indian flag colors everywhere
- decorative mandalas
- generic cultural imagery
- patriotic graphics
- cliché India stock photos

The Indian identity should come from the JOURNALISM and imagery, not decoration.

60% information / 40% visual content.
