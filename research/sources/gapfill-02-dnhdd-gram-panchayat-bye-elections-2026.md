# NOTIFICATION

Whereas General Elections to Panchayats and Municipal Councils in the Union Territory of 
Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu were held in October / November. 2025;

And whereas, there was no contesting candidate in the following wards of Gram Panchayats in 
Dadra and Nagar Haveli District during the aforesaid General Elections: -

| S. No. | Name of the Gram Panchayat | Ward No. | Seat whether general or reserved |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Galonda | Ward No 4 | Scheduled Tribes Women |
| 2 | Galonda | Ward No 7 | Scheduled Tribes Women |
| 3 | Khanvel | Ward No 6 | Un-reserved |
| 4 | Khanvel | Ward No 7 | Women |
| 5 | Khanvel | Ward No 8 | Un-reserved |
| 6 | Khanvel | Ward No 9 | Scheduled Tribes Women |
| 7 | Randha | Ward No 5 | Scheduled Tribes Women |
| 8 | Vasona | Ward No 1 | Women |
| 9 | Vasona | Ward No 2 | Scheduled Tribes Women |
| 10 | Vasona | Ward No 3 | Scheduled Tribes Women |
| 11 | Vasona | Ward No 6 | Scheduled Tribes Women |

Now, therefore, in pursuance of the provisions of Rule 39 (2) read with Rule 29 of the Dadra 
and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu Panchayats (Election Procedures) Rules, 2023 the Election 
Commission for Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu hereby calls upon the said wards to elect 
a member each to fill the said vacancies in accordance with the provision of Dadra and Nagar Haveli 
and Daman & Diu Panchayats Regulation, 2012 and the said Rules and Orders made thereunder,

With respect to the said bye election, the Election Commission hereby appoints the following 
dates:-

a) the 16<sup>th</sup> January, 2026 (Friday), between 11.00 a.m. and 3.00 p.m., as the last date and hours 
for making nominations;
