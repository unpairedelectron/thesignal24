# State-by-state reservation and representation coverage

**Research cutoff:** 10 November 2026  
**Scope:** 28 states and 8 Union Territories, using the existing Signal 24 profile structure. Every newly researched timeline item is bilingual and links to its source. Source registry: `research/sources.json`.

## Findings

All 36 jurisdictions now have a state profile. The research distinguishes enacted rules, court decisions, interim orders, committee work, Cabinet/Assembly action, allegations, and recruitment notices. A source-backed process or an explicitly labeled evidence gap is not presented as a new quota.

### State and UT coverage

- **Andhra Pradesh:** Supreme Court did not restore the struck-down 34% BC local-body quota; later 50%-capped arrangements need separate verification.
- **Arunachal Pradesh:** 80:20 job-reservation review recommendations were reported; adoption was not verified.
- **Assam:** Assembly resolution sought faster women’s-reservation rollout; it did not itself change seat allocation.
- **Bihar:** A November High Court ruling addressed recruitment eligibility in a specific case.
- **Chhattisgarh:** Cabinet approval for a former-Agniveer quota was reported; implementation notification was not verified.
- **Goa:** Interim order restrained notification of estimated ST population; it did not directly stay reservation.
- **Gujarat:** High Court protected benefits already lawfully secured after a later SC-list change; appeal status is unknown.
- **Haryana:** High Court upheld a policy for in-service doctors seeking reserved postgraduate seats.
- **Himachal Pradesh:** Reported High Court ruling addressed caste-reservation eligibility after migration through marriage; appeal status is unknown.
- **Jharkhand:** The last located record is the Governor’s 2023 return of a proposed 77% state-job reservation bill; current status is unverified.
- **Karnataka:** A community association urged implementation of the State Backward Classes Commission survey; government adoption was not confirmed.
- **Kerala:** High Court sought an update on the OBC socio-economic survey; compliance with the November deadline was not verified.
- **Madhya Pradesh:** High Court kept OBC reservation at 14%; a state Supreme Court appeal was reported, with later status unknown.
- **Maharashtra:** High Court resumed hearing challenges to the 2024 10% Maratha quota; no final ruling was reported.
- **Manipur:** October Gazette amendment set panchayat SC/ST and women’s reservation rules.
- **Meghalaya:** An 80% ST split appeared in a review report; adoption and final Assembly disposition were not verified.
- **Mizoram:** Official second provisional NEET-UG state-quota merit list was published.
- **Nagaland:** Five Tribes committee sought NPSC clarification on Backward Tribe post allocation.
- **Odisha:** State formed a commission to study SEBC representation in local bodies; this is not a new quota.
- **Punjab:** NCSC notice followed an allegation about SC reservation in lecturer recruitment; it is not a finding.
- **Rajasthan:** Cabinet acceptance of an OBC local-body representation report was reported; ward-wise implementation remains unverified.
- **Sikkim:** High Court distinguished Central and State OBC list eligibility in a case-specific ruling.
- **Tamil Nadu:** Cabinet approved a Social Justice Commission for a survey; operation had not been confirmed.
- **Telangana:** State urged BC/OBC enumeration in the Union Census; no state quota change was established.
- **Tripura:** TTAADC village committee election turnout and voting figures were reported; seat-reservation changes were not established.
- **Uttar Pradesh:** Allahabad High Court ordered panchayat elections under the existing reservation framework; later compliance remains unknown.
- **Uttarakhand:** High Court upheld 50% women’s reservation in student-union polls; appeal status is unknown.
- **West Bengal:** Assembly passed two OBC bills; assent, Gazette publication and operative details were not verified.
- **Andaman and Nicobar Islands:** An official recruitment notice applied OBC-NCL requirements to those posts only.
- **Chandigarh:** Local reporting described municipal ward reservations; the final official order was not confirmed.
- **Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu:** Official Gazette listed reserved seats for specific 2026 panchayat by-elections; this is not a territory-wide quota change.
- **Delhi:** High Court declined to halt Town Vending Committee elections in an OBC-reservation dispute without deciding the merits.
- **Jammu and Kashmir:** Reservation-review recommendations awaited competent-authority approval.
- **Ladakh:** Administration reported a draw for women’s reservation in LAHDC-Sankoo elections; final ward roster was not confirmed.
- **Lakshadweep:** Official recruitment index listed 2026 notices but did not establish a reservation change; this remains a documented evidence gap.
- **Puducherry:** Madras High Court addressed reservation-certificate eligibility through a mother in a case-specific ruling.

## Editorial limitations

Some records rely on reputable secondary reporting because the underlying judgment, Gazette, or order was not extractable. These entries are narrowly worded and link the source used. Later orders, appeals, implementation notices, report publication, and election schedules should be checked before treating a pending status as current. Lakshadweep has no verified recent reservation-specific change in the material reviewed; Jharkhand’s latest located bill-status record is from 2023.

No fabricated population estimates or unsupported claims of enacted quotas were used. Existing archive-backed profiles retain their prior material and receive one dated current development where verified.
