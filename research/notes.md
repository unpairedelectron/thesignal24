### Northern states and Union Territories

Profiles cover the 10 listed jurisdictions. Recent records include an Uttar Pradesh panchayat-election deadline, a Haryana PG-seat policy ruling, a Punjab NCSC notice (allegation only), Himachal migrant-reservation eligibility, Uttarakhand women’s student-election quota, Rajasthan local-body OBC report, Delhi vending-committee dispute, Chandigarh ward allocation reporting, Jammu and Kashmir’s pending review, and Ladakh’s women’s-seat draw. Appeal/compliance status remains open for several cases.

### Eastern states and island territories

Profiles cover Bihar, Jharkhand, Odisha, West Bengal, Sikkim and Andaman and Nicobar Islands. Bihar and Sikkim have case-specific High Court rulings; Odisha formed an SEBC representation panel; West Bengal passed bills whose assent and operative status remain unverified. Jharkhand’s last located bill-status evidence is from 2023, and the present status is unknown. The Andaman recruitment notice confirms OBC-NCL use for that recruitment only.

### Western and central states

Profiles cover Gujarat, Maharashtra, Goa, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh and Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu. Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh have 2026 court rulings; Maharashtra’s 10% Maratha quota challenge is pending; Goa’s ST-population notification was temporarily stayed; Chhattisgarh’s Cabinet approval is not confirmed as implemented. The UT Gazette records specific panchayat by-election seat reservations.

### Southern states and Union Territories

Profiles cover Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Puducherry and Lakshadweep. Andhra’s 34% BC local-body quota remains struck down in the cited appeal; later capped arrangements need source verification. Karnataka’s survey implementation remains contested, Kerala’s affidavit deadline passed without confirmed compliance, and Tamil Nadu’s survey commission was approved but not yet reported operational. Puducherry has a case-specific eligibility ruling. Lakshadweep’s current recruitment index surfaced no reservation change.

### Northeastern states

Profiles cover Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland and Tripura. Manipur’s Gazette amended panchayat reservation rules; Assam adopted a resolution; Meghalaya’s proposed split remains unverified as enacted; Nagaland’s Backward Tribe allocation is under review. Mizoram and Tripura records document a provisional state-quota merit list and local election results, respectively.

## Conflicts & Open Questions

- Research cutoff: 10 November 2026. Several items are procedural, provisional, proposals, reports, or allegations—not enacted changes or final decisions.
- Jharkhand, Lakshadweep, and parts of the Andaman recruitment framework remain evidence gaps; do not imply that a search gap means no policy exists.
- Verify later court orders, appeals, Gazette notifications, election schedules, and commission report implementation before treating any pending status as current.

## Gaps

- The 36 profile facts link to source pages. Several primary court/Gazette records were not fully extractable; where secondary reports were used, the page labels the source and narrows the claim.
- No unsupported population estimates or claims of new quotas were added.
